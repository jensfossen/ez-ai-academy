# Foundations Course Index

## Outcome

Build a clear mental model one idea at a time. Do not expose the full vocabulary map at the beginning.

## Released modules

1. **What is an LLM?** Read `module-01-llm.md`.

## Later modules

Add agents, context, harnesses, and evaluation loops only in their own modules. A later module may refer back to the LLM mental model, but Module 1 must not pre-teach those terms.

Design history: [`models-landscape-outline.md`](models-landscape-outline.md).
